Methodology — how every number is made

Coverage. The board ranks every protocol in the sealed snapshot that records no token (gecko_id and symbol both null) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades — that is the 40-protocol watchlist (TVL at or above the $50M floor) plus every rankable protocol below it.

Implied value. implied_value_usd = round(power_law_base(tvl_usd) × category_offset × momentum_factor × maturity_factor), clamped to $1,000,000 – $100,000,000,000,000. The effective multiple shown per row is power_law_base × category_offset / tvl_usd, so the dashboard recomputes the same value as tvl_usd × comparable_multiple × momentum_factor × maturity_factor.

  • The size curve. The market-cap/TVL multiple is not flat — it falls about 20× from the smallest to the largest TVL. Across the 128 tokened protocols, the median multiple by TVL band is: under $10M TVL, 0.65; $10–50M, 0.37; $50–250M, 0.17; $250M–$1B, 0.08; over $1B, 0.03. A flat multiple therefore overvalues large protocols and undervalues small ones by several times. The board instead fits a power law by ordinary least squares on the 128 tokened protocols in this snapshot: log(mcap) = + × log(TVL). The slope is far below 1.0 — market cap grows much slower than TVL — and it is stable: fitting the two halves of the tokened set separately gives slopes of 0.45 and 0.59, both well below 1.0. The curve is fitted at runtime from this snapshot; nothing is hardcoded.
  • power_law_base — exp(intercept + slope × log(tvl_usd)): the value the size curve gives for this protocol's TVL alone.
  • category_offset — the geometric mean of actual mcap / power-law implied mcap for the tokened protocols in the same category, shrunk halfway toward the global offset (which is ~1.0 by construction): offset = global × (category / global)^0.5. A category with fewer than 10 tokened comparables uses the global offset instead, so one outlier never sets the value. A comparable must be a token that actually trades at a positive value: protocols whose market cap is 0 or negative are dead tokens and are left out. Zero hardcoded names, zero hardcoded prices.
  • momentum_factor — clamp(1 + change_7d / 100 × 0.1, 0.8, 1.5). A protocol whose TVL grew 20% in 7 days scores 1.02; one that fell 20% scores 0.98. No reported 7-day change is neutral, 1.0. The weight is deliberately small: growth is a timing signal, not the value itself.
  • maturity_factor — 0.9 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.1. Age is the days from listed_at to the snapshot date; a protocol with no listed_at uses the snapshot's median age. Long-running and growing ranks higher, but only slightly — the factor moves the value by at most 10%.

Verdicts. Farm now — implied value $25M or more. Watch — $5M or more. Skip — below $5M. Each plan opens with the one decisive reason for its verdict.

Farm plans. Every step is generated from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL band and age: the action, the principal (TVL band $250–$2,000), the gas cost for its chain, and the time. Ethereum L1 runs about $2–$15 a transaction, L2s about $0.05–$0.50, Solana about $0.01. Each plan is netted to its break-even: the gas of one setup transaction, one a week through the hold, and the claim, over the active hours the steps name — so the allocation must be worth at least that much per hour to cover its cost. The capital stays in the position, so it is not counted as a cost.

Pre-token vs already trading. The board is strictly pre-token. Protocols whose token already trades appear only in the “Already trading” view, and only as the protocols the size curve is fitted from — never mixed into the ranking.

Determinism. All figures are computed from the sealed snapshot; the program runs twice and both runs write identical bytes. Language discipline: a protocol “records no token” — the snapshot is DefiLlama's record, not proof that no token exists.
