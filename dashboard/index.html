Methodology — how every number is made

Coverage. The board ranks every protocol in the sealed snapshot that records no token (gecko_id and symbol both null) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades — that is the 40-protocol watchlist (TVL at or above the $50M floor) plus every rankable protocol below it.

Implied value. implied_value_usd = round(tvl_usd × comparable_multiple × momentum_factor × maturity_factor), clamped to $1,000,000 – $100,000,000,000,000.

  • comparable_multiple — the median mcap_usd / tvl_usd of the tokened protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot at runtime. A category with fewer than 3 tokened comparables uses the global median instead, so one outlier never sets the multiple. Zero hardcoded names, zero hardcoded prices.
  • momentum_factor — clamp(1 + change_7d / 100 × 0.5, 0.8, 1.5). A protocol whose TVL grew 20% in 7 days scores 1.10; one that fell 20% scores 0.90. No reported 7-day change is neutral, 1.0.
  • maturity_factor — 0.6 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4. Age is the days from listed_at to the snapshot date; a protocol with no listed_at uses the snapshot's median age. Long-running and growing ranks higher.

Verdicts. Farm now — implied value $25M or more. Watch — $5M or more. Skip — below $5M. Each plan opens with the one decisive reason for its verdict.

Farm plans. Every step is generated from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL band and age: the action, the principal (TVL band $250–$2,000), the gas cost for its chain, and the time. Ethereum L1 runs about $2–$15 a transaction, L2s about $0.05–$0.50, Solana about $0.01.

Pre-token vs already trading. The board is strictly pre-token. Protocols whose token already trades appear only in the “Already trading” view, and only as the comparables the multiples come from — never mixed into the ranking.

Determinism. All figures are computed from the sealed snapshot; the program runs twice and both runs write identical bytes. Language discipline: a protocol “records no token” — the snapshot is DefiLlama's record, not proof that no token exists.
