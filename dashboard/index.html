Which protocol launches next — and what is it worth?

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks that records no token, ranked by the implied value of its launch. The value follows from the snapshot's own figures: TVL through a fitted size curve (a large protocol's market cap is a much smaller multiple of its TVL than a small one's), and the launch odds from the peers' record — how often protocols with this age, size and growth have launched a token. Methodology — how every number is made

Coverage. The board ranks every protocol in the sealed snapshot that records no token (gecko_id and symbol both null) and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades — that is the 40-protocol watchlist (TVL at or above the $50M floor) plus every rankable protocol below it.

Accounting — all 758 protocols are counted.

Held-out test.

Walk-forward test.

Keep or drop — every value term tested on both held-out tests. A term stays only if it improves held-out error on both tests. Median |log error| first, mean |log error| second; the standard error of a median over 128 protocols is about 0.15 log units, so gains of a few thousandths are noise, not wins.

The previous version of this radar shipped all three terms; this version cuts them because the tests say they add nothing.

Uncertainty intervals.

A split-conformal 80% interval was checked separately (4 rotations, fit 64 / calibrate 32 / test 32): mean coverage 0.789 against a nominal 0.80 — the same as the P10–P90 band's 0.797 — so the leave-one-out residual band is kept as the row range, and the conformal check stands as a second validation.

Implied value. implied_value_usd = round(power_law_base(tvl_usd)), clamped to $1,000,000 – $100,000,000,000,000. The effective multiple shown per row is power_law_base / tvl_usd, so the dashboard recomputes the same value as tvl_usd × comparable_multiple.

  • The size curve. The market-cap/TVL multiple is not flat — it falls about 20× from the smallest to the largest TVL. Across the 128 tokened protocols, the median multiple by TVL band is: under $10M TVL, 0.65; $10–50M, 0.37; $50–250M, 0.17; $250M–$1B, 0.08; over $1B, 0.03. A flat multiple therefore overvalues large protocols and undervalues small ones by several times. The board instead fits a power law by ordinary least squares on the 128 tokened protocols in this snapshot: log(mcap) = + × log(TVL). The slope is far below 1.0 — market cap grows much slower than TVL — and it is stable: fitting the two halves of the tokened set separately gives slopes of 0.45 and 0.59, both well below 1.0. The curve is fitted at runtime from this snapshot; nothing is hardcoded.
  • power_law_base — exp(intercept + slope × log(tvl_usd)): the value the size curve gives for this protocol's TVL alone. A comparable must be a token that actually trades at a positive value: protocols whose market cap is 0 or negative are dead tokens and are left out of the fit. Zero hardcoded names, zero hardcoded prices.
  • Launch odds. Each row's odds are the fitted probability that a protocol with this age, TVL and 7-day growth has launched a token by now — a logistic regression on age, log(TVL) and change_7d, fitted at runtime on the 442 launch candidates the snapshot records (244 tokened, 198 pre-token). 7-day growth is winsorised to ±100% (one candidate records +4,448%, an outlier that would otherwise dominate the feature). The expected value beside the implied value is implied_value_usd × launch_odds. The ranking stays by implied value: the odds are the peers' cross-sectional record, not a forward forecast — the snapshot carries no token launch dates, so no forward launch hazard is claimed. A Weibull current-status fit was checked for a forward hazard and rejected: its shape is unstable across splits (k from 0.10 to 1.63, even flipping direction), so the forward hazard cannot be pinned down from this snapshot.

Verdicts. Farm now — implied value $25M or more. Watch — $5M or more. Skip — below $5M. Each plan opens with the one decisive reason for its verdict. Each row also shows its expected value (implied × launch odds) so a reader can weigh the launch value against how often protocols like it have launched.

Farm plans. Every step is generated from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL band and age: the action, the principal (TVL band $250–$2,000), the gas cost for its chain, and the time. Ethereum L1 runs about $2–$15 a transaction, L2s about $0.05–$0.50, Solana about $0.01. Each plan is netted to its break-even: the gas of one setup transaction, one a week through the hold, and the claim, over the active hours the steps name — so the allocation must be worth at least that much per hour to cover its cost. The capital stays in the position, so it is not counted as a cost.

Pre-token vs already trading. The board is strictly pre-token. Protocols whose token already trades appear only in the “Already trading” view, and only as the protocols the size curve is fitted from — never mixed into the ranking.

Determinism. All figures are computed from the sealed snapshot; the program runs twice and both runs write identical bytes. Language discipline: a protocol “records no token” — the snapshot is DefiLlama's record, not proof that no token exists.
